Oticon More 3

₹281000

More 3  miniRITE

Oticon More is built on the innovative Polaris™ platform, which uses a deep neural network to rapidly and optimally manage incoming sounds based on individual needs. Oticon More features Bluetooth wireless technology for seamless connectivity with your favourite devices. 

MoreSound Intelligence – after scanning and analysing the sound scene 500 times per second to understand the complexity of the sound scene, the hearing aid can organize the sounds around you to create clear contrast and balance between sounds. 

MoreSound Amplifier – analyses details in sound, and optimally amplifies them for the brain to have access to relevant information. 

Rechargeable batteries – get charged for a full day of hearing, including streaming, in just 3 hours. Quick recharge of 30 minutes can last you six hours of power.
